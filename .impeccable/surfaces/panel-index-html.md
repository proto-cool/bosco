# Surface: bosco.proto.cool (panel/index.html)

Scope: the whole public page. Visitor mode: Read. The visitor is a Bluesky
bystander who wants to understand in a minute that a real fruit fly brain is
acting here, and what it is doing now; audit readers get the receipts in the
same column. No task, no controls: the page only watches him.

Audience and job: understand what he is and what he is doing now; then, if
curious, see what he learned and what he said. Proof: the live activity
file, the ledger-derived status, his real posts by URI. Constraints: PRODUCT.md
(read-only, no stored text of others, no fabricated liveliness, he is "he",
lowercase fly voice, ARC UI as the tool not the subject).

What Nick called noise (2026-09-14): panels of different sizes; too many
numbers. Keep: the brain leads. Nothing else sacred.

## Direction contract

THESIS: One specimen under glass and a label column. The brain owns the left
two thirds of the screen for the whole scroll and never leaves; everything
else is a single rail of same-height rows beside it. It refuses the
dashboard-of-tiles: no grid of unequal cards, no stat wall, no card that
competes with the specimen.

OWN-WORLD: ARC dark ground (`--bg-deep`) with no card surfaces at all: rows
are separated by one hairline (`--divider`), never boxed. One accent
(`--accent-primary`) for the live dot, the current projection word, and the
readout fill; `--accent-secondary` only for aversion. Labels in the label
context, figures in the numeral context, body in body. Brain groups reduce
to three colours: mushroom body (Kenyon cells, output, dopamine) in the
accent, descending/motor in the secondary, everything else in muted text.
Light is one ambient lobe behind the brain, none elsewhere. With the
content removed the page is a dark field, a lit cloud of dots on the left,
and a ladder of hairlines on the right.

STORY: I see a brain, lit, moving, with three captions. I read down the
rail: what he wants to do now, what he did today, what he remembers, what he
said, then the record and the explanation. I leave knowing he is real, he
acted on his own, and he is learning slowly.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: a thin top line (name, handle, live dot, "in
development"). Below, left 8/12: the canvas filling the viewport height,
three captions in its top-left corner (neurons firing, kenyon cells active,
his time), three projection words under it (front · top · side), no legend
buttons; groups are named in one caption line. Right 4/12: the rail begins
with "now": five readout lines with hairline tracks and a threshold tick;
then "today": one figure (silence) and one sentence with the counts inline.
The rail continues below the fold with memory, people, what he said,
windows, what this is. Phone: the canvas at 4:3 on top, not sticky (a pinned 4:3 canvas would cover most of a phone screen while the rail scrolls under it), the rail beneath it at full width.

FORM: Specimen and rail, index 3 of 7 on my ordered list (specimen sheet,
strata, specimen and rail, lab log, timeline ribbon, single-number ladder,
tabbed instrument). Seed key cc1eb193; the roll assigned the ladder; Nick
chose the rail from the dealt hand.

Signature interaction: the rail scrolls, the brain stays lit; the current
row's label brightens to `--text-primary` as it passes the top of the rail.
Motion grammar: decay of spikes on the canvas, threshold-crossing glow on a
readout line, nothing else; reduced motion shortens decay and removes
transitions.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.

Unresolved: whether the dunce account joins this page; whether day-by-day
