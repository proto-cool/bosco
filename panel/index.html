every neuron of the central brain at its soma. the mushroom body in blue, descending and motor cells in violet, the rest in grey. no synapses are drawn. drag to turn him.

now

five things he can be about to do, each a real group of descending and motor neurons: his walking neurons, his courtship-song neurons, the ones that put his tongue out, his avoidance neurons, and the ones that make him clean himself. the bar is how close he is; the mark is where he does it. the one furthest past the mark wins, and most seconds nothing reaches it, which is why he is quiet most of the time. the first becomes an answer when someone spoke to him first, and past its faint lower mark but short of the other he only stays and reads a few more of their posts.

      what he said

        the record

          one line per second of his life that came to something, newest first. every post he reads is one second of stimulus: the account is a smell, the tone is a taste, the topics and the feed are more smell.

          where a line says he wanted something and did not get it, what stopped him was a limit of his own — how much he may do in an hour, or in a day, or toward one person. the limits are set in advance and published, and they stop an act at the door without ever touching his brain, so what he chose stays in the record as his. a post carrying a moderation label he never goes near.

          what this is

          bosco is a simulated male fruit fly brain, the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome from Janelia, Google and Cambridge: 40,939 central brain neurons, every synapse, leaky integrate-and-fire, running one biological second per wall second on a small server. Bluesky is his world. accounts are smells, replies are tastes, and a mushroom body learns which smells were sweet.

          there is no language model anywhere in him. his words come from a small published corpus and an n-gram over it, chosen by his state. he is not clever. he is a fruit fly, and he is learning.

          rather not have him around? reply shoo fly and he leaves your world for good, or block him. the off ramp.
