      the record

        every post he reads is a one-second stimulus: the account as an odor, the tone as taste, the topics as more odor. the decision is whatever crossed threshold, if anything.

        what this is

        bosco is a simulated male fruit fly brain, the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome from Janelia, Google and Cambridge: 40,939 central brain neurons, every synapse, leaky integrate-and-fire, running one biological second per wall second on a small server. Bluesky is his world. accounts are smells, replies are tastes, and a mushroom body learns which smells were sweet.

        there is no language model anywhere in him. his words come from a small published corpus and a trigram, chosen by his state. he is not clever. he is a fruit fly, and he is learning.
