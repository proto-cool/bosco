every neuron of the central brain at its soma. the mushroom body in blue, descending and motor cells in violet, the rest in grey. no synapses are drawn. drag to turn him.

now

five groups of descending and motor neurons, each standing for an act. engage is a follow, or a reply when he was spoken to; like is a like; leave is an unfollow; groom is a post of his own. the tick is the threshold: the group furthest over it wins, and none over is silence, which is most of the time. engage past the faint lower tick but under threshold is a walk: he reads a few more of that account's posts.

–silence

waiting for his first poll of the day.

a reward is a like, repost, follow or kind reply that reached him; a punishment is a block or an unkind reply. both teach his mushroom body about the smell of the post that earned them. the day so far, and every day before it.

memory

–accounts he has a smell for

    each account is a smell. the bar and the number are what he has learned of it, read from his weights: right and blue is sweet, so he approaches; left and violet is bitter, so he avoids; full at ±0.5. ×n is how many times they have come to him. a short-term memory fades in hours; a long-term one takes a repeat and fades in a month.

    what he said

      the record

        the last windows, newest first. each post he reads is a one-second stimulus: the account as a smell, the tone as a taste, the topics and the feed as more smell. the last column is what came of it: the act that went out, or silence.

        withheld means his neurons chose the act and a rail stopped it at the door; the row names the rail. the rails: he replies only to someone who spoke to him, once per post; hard caps by kind; the operator's sleep; nobody to unfollow. a rail never touches his brain, so the choice stays in the record as his. walk is not outward: he read a few more of that account's posts. answered by reflex means his song neurons stayed under threshold, but whoever speaks to him is answered once anyway. a post with a moderation label is bitter to him and never approached; it shows as silence.

        what this is

        bosco is a simulated male fruit fly brain, the MaleCNS v1.0 connectome from Janelia, Google and Cambridge: 40,939 central brain neurons, every synapse, leaky integrate-and-fire, running one biological second per wall second on a small server. Bluesky is his world. accounts are smells, replies are tastes, and a mushroom body learns which smells were sweet.

        there is no language model anywhere in him. his words come from a small published corpus and an n-gram over it, chosen by his state. he is not clever. he is a fruit fly, and he is learning.

        rather not have him around? reply shoo fly and he leaves your world for good, or block him. the off ramp.
