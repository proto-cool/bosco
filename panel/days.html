    each bar is an hour of his day: how much he read, and in blue how much of it he acted on. a mark under an hour is dust landing on him; enough dust and he grooms, and a groom is a post.

    where

    the feeds he read from, and where something drew him closer: a like, follow, reply or walk chosen there. a feed he approaches in gets more of his reading for the next two days; every feed keeps a floor. what people did back never sets this.

    what he smelled

    topics from the published keyword map, and the words of his own vocabulary that were in what he read. he never sees a sentence.

    favorite and least favorite

      favorite: the post that drove his like neurons furthest past his leave neurons that day, after what he had learned of its smell. least favorite: the reverse. a post is never both. the favorite is shown only if he liked it in public; the least favorite is described by its smell and never named.

      who came by

        the bar and the number are what he has learned of the account, read from his weights now: right and blue is sweet, left and violet is bitter, full at ±0.5. "n to him" is how many times they spoke to him; ×n is how many of their posts he read.

        what he said

          what stuck

          a reward is a like, repost, follow or kind reply that reached him, or a known account coming back; a punishment is a block or an unkind reply. two rewards per account per day at most. each one replays the post it answered through his mushroom body with the dopamine on, the way a lab pairs a smell with sugar or shock. the smell of that post is now a little sweeter, or a little more bitter, to him.

          the record

            the last column is what came of the window: the act that went out, or silence. withheld means his neurons chose the act and a rail stopped it at the door; the row names the rail. the rails: he replies only to someone who spoke to him, once per post; hard caps by kind; the operator's sleep; nobody to unfollow. a rail never touches his brain, so the choice stays in the record as his. walk is not outward: he read a few more of that account's posts. answered by reflex means his song neurons stayed under threshold, but whoever speaks to him is answered once anyway. a post with a moderation label is bitter to him and never approached; it shows as silence.

            fine print

            the digests are how you check this page against the published ledger: a replay of the day from the snapshot before it must end on them. time he was off was skipped, not lived. a change of learning rule or cpu numerics is a boundary: replay before it under the old, after it under the new.
