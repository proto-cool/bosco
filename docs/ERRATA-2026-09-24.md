## 2026-09-25: the maze loss's clamp (A4 pilot)

`a4_pilot.maze_loss` clamps logits to ±30 without centring them per item. In the A4 broad
preflight this zeroed every gradient once a shared drift pushed all logits past −30
(`docs/A4-BROAD.md`, amendment 1). The A4 pilot ran the same loss at a smaller read scale
(≈70); its loss kept falling, so it trained, but part of its batches may have been
clamped. Its G1 conclusion is not changed by this alone: the plain net and the nose-alone
baseline, which led the result, are not brains with a read scale; A4 broad reruns the
brains with the fixed loss. The pilot's code
is left as run.

## 2026-09-25: from the code audit (`docs/audit-2026-09-25/code.md`)

- **The controls were not scaled like the real brain.** Each control divided its input
  weights by the *real* brain's per-neuron input totals, but the shuffles move synapses
  to new targets. Summed input weight per neuron averages 0.458 in the real brain, 0.748
  in layered and 4.75 in free. The layered shuffle also has fewer trainable KC→MBON
  synapses (54,324 vs 61,210) and free has only 1,710. **Every real-vs-shuffle
  comparison (A1, A2, A5 D2, A4 broad) is confounded, direction unknown.** "The wiring
  does not matter on these tasks" is withdrawn until the controls are rebuilt with their
  own normalisation.
- **Most of the brain was silent and untrainable.** At A4 broad's start, 91% of neurons
  were silent, including every descending neuron. With `tanh(relu)` there is no gradient
  below threshold, so 7,046 of 8,257 cell types never changed in training. In trained
  A5, 76% of neurons are silent. **Every result so far comes from a brain mostly switched
  off.**
- **The KC sparseness penalty was inert.** Its target (0.01) is a mean rate, while the
  start target and the D3 bar count active cells. The penalty was zero throughout, and
  A4 broad ended with 15% of KCs active.
- **The A4 broad smoke run scored the sealed cold test** (300 items, the real brain
  0.434, layered 0.333) and wrote the picks to `runs/a4-broad/smoke/` before the full
  run. The number was seen in the log. No decision or rule changed because of it, but
  it is a look at the sealed set, as in the GATE-A2 erratum. Smoke runs must not score
  sealed sets.
