# Audit: where the simulated fly is not the fly (2026-09-24)

Nick, after learning that the fly's vision was never in the model: "Stop all
your work and audit yourself for other stupid gaps like this." Every run was
stopped (A2b killed before it finished; nothing is running). Each item below
was checked against the MaleCNS data and the code on 2026-09-24, not recalled.
Ordered by how much it undermines "the fly decides".

## 1. He has no vision. The central brain's visual inputs were cut.

- The model keeps 40,939 of MaleCNS's 211,577 bodies (`model.CB_SUPERCLASSES`).
  Left out: the optic lobes (89,403 `ol_intrinsic`), photoreceptors (6,098
  `ol_sensory`), and **all 9,201 `visual_projection` neurons**, plus 563
  `visual_centrifugal`. The visual projection neurons (LC, LPLC and the rest)
  are how vision *enters* the central brain. Without them, the central brain
  we simulate receives no visual signal at all.
- Since v1, pictures have been injected straight into 335 visual Kenyon cells
  (and in A2b, into all of them). That is a side door, not fly vision. A2's
  0.78 on pictures measured that side door.
- The connectivity is in the local data (`connectome-weights` holds every
  body), so it can be fixed. Adding the 9,201 projection neurons is modest.
  Adding the optic lobes is about 100k more neurons.

## 2. Sentences are wired onto real glomeruli arbitrarily, including innate ones.

- The antenna sends principal component k of the sentence to the k-th
  glomerulus **in alphabetical order** (`gateb3.Antenna`, `orn_index`): D, DA1,
  DA2, … V, … VM7d. VM7v is left out.
- Some of those glomeruli carry **hard-wired innate meaning**, through the
  lateral horn: DA1 (cVA, the male pheromone), V (CO₂, innate avoidance), DA2
  (geosmin, innate avoidance), VA1v (female pheromones). Every sentence drives
  those pathways by the accident of the alphabet.
- All ORNs of a glomerulus, left and right, get the same drive.

## 3. Sweet and bitter never touch his sense of taste.

- The fly's actual sweet/bitter circuit is taste: 83 sugar and 57 bitter
  gustatory neurons are in the model, and the sugar-to-proboscis pathway was
  reproduced in phase 1 (`phase1-shiu-gate.md`). No sweet/bitter gate since
  v1 has used them. The question has gone through smell and the mushroom body.

## 4. The answer is not read from his behaviour.

- Since C1, the answer has been approach MBONs minus avoid MBONs, grouped by
  which dopamine neurons share their compartment. That is a read of the
  mushroom body's *memory output*, not of what he does.
- His behaviour outputs are in the model and unused: proboscis motor neurons
  (MN9 and others: *eat*), walking and turning descending neurons (DNp09,
  DNa02: *approach*), escape (DNp01, the giant fibre: *flee*).
  `config/readout_populations.yaml` has them from v1.

## 5. The trained brain is a generic rate network wearing the wiring.

- A1/A2 replace Shiu's spiking neurons with `tanh(relu(·))` rate units, 12
  steps, no time constants per cell type, and each input normalised by the
  cell's total synapses.
- 82k per-neuron gains and thresholds are trained freely. That is enough for
  training to override most of what the wiring would impose, which is why
  scrambled brains match or beat him (A1, A2).

## 6. What he learns is not learned the fly's way.

- A1/A2 learn by gradient descent over the whole brain. The dopamine neurons,
  the fly's teaching signal, play no part in learning in the rate model; they
  are ordinary excitatory cells in it.

## 7. Neuromodulators are treated as fast synapses.

- The 449 dopamine, octopamine and serotonin neurons (384 + 36 + 29) are
  modelled as fast excitatory synapses (Shiu's convention). In a fly they
  modulate, over seconds, through receptors that are not in the connectome.

## 8. Transmitter signs are conventions, not facts.

- Glutamate is always inhibitory (6,955 neurons). It is inhibitory through
  GluClα in much of the fly brain but excitatory at some synapses.
- 1,247 neurons with an "unclear" or "unknown" transmitter default to
  excitatory.

## 9. Not in any connectome, and so not here

Electrical synapses (gap junctions), neuropeptides and hormones, glia
(11,864 glial bodies are in MaleCNS; all are excluded, as are 15,893 orphan
fragments). Every connectome simulation shares this limit; it is listed so
it is not forgotten.

## 10. My process

- I built and ran gates for a week on a model whose anatomy I had not
  audited, and I said things were "true to the fly" (the 0.78 on pictures)
  without checking what the model contained.
- Every harness failure so far was found only after a run: the CLIP cone
  (B), the 0.5 threshold (B3), the lifetime rule read (C1), vision (now).
  From now on, each gate's pre-registration carries an anatomy check: which
  real circuits the task uses, and whether they are in the model.

## What fixing it would mean, in order

1. **Vision:** add the 9,201 visual projection neurons and feed pictures into
   them. The optic lobes after that, if needed (also Doom).
2. **Taste and behaviour:** read the answer from what he *does* (proboscis,
   approach, flee), and let sweet/bitter reach the taste pathway.
3. **Smell mapping:** keep sentences off the innate glomeruli (DA1, V, DA2,
   VA1v and the like), or map deliberately rather than alphabetically.
4. **The trained brain (5–6):** decide whether per-neuron training is how
   Bosco learns, or whether the fly's own dopamine teaching signal carries
   the learning. That is the choice between "a network shaped like a fly"
   and "a fly". It is Nick's to make.
