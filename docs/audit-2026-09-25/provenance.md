# Data provenance ledger (2026-09-25)

Written for decision 10 in `docs/DECISIONS-2026-09-25.md`: "Data is ethically sourced, with provenance all the way through: a ledger per dataset and per encoder before training." It covers every dataset and model that bosco v2 has used or plans to use. Nothing in code or earlier docs was changed.

**Method.** Each licence was read at the URL given: a dataset card or the HF API, a GitHub LICENSE file, the paper, or the host's terms. **"unverified"** means no licence could be found at the source. It never means a guess. Where a Hugging Face mirror's licence tag disagrees with the original, the original wins. Research was done on 2026-09-25. Nothing here is legal advice.

**Recommendations.** The bar is "as ethically sourced as possible, with provenance the whole way", for a decision service that may become commercial.
- **KEEP**: the licence is verified and permissive. The text is not scraped from private individuals. The labour is documented or benign.
- **REVIEW**: one named thing has to be checked or fixed first.
- **DROP**: any of the following:
  - the licence is missing or unverified;
  - the text is real people's posts scraped without consent, especially hateful posts;
  - the terms forbid redistribution or commercial use of a training source;
  - the text or labels were generated by an LLM (this clashes with "no LLM anywhere").

Test-only sets (BTZSC, Jev bench) are judged slightly more leniently on non-commercial terms, since evaluation during research is what those terms allow. They are **not** excused from no-redistribution clauses or from scraping in breach of a site's terms.

## Summary

| group | items | KEEP | REVIEW | DROP |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| tasksource kinds (A4 / A4b training) | 44 | 5 | 7 | 32 |
| BTZSC cold-test tasks | 22 | 9 | 7 | 6 |
| A2/A3/A5 and Jev-bench datasets | 10 | 3 | 3 | 4 |
| encoders | 9 | 0 | 3 | 6 |
| connectome and model code | 3 | 2 | 1 | 0 |
| proposed: Wikipedia, Wikidata | 2 | 1 | 1 | 0 |

Main problems:
1. **Most of the 44 training kinds fail.** 32 are DROP:
   - 13 are tweets. X's developer terms forbid redistributing tweet text, and a mirror's MIT or Apache tag cannot change that.
   - Others are scraped forum or comment posts, much of it hate: Stormfront, YouTube, Reddit and Gab-adjacent sources.
   - Others carry non-commercial or no-redistribution terms (SEMAINE, SwDA, MRDA, HatEval, Kaggle, Yahoo Webscope).
   - Others are copyrighted scripts or news, or have no licence at all.
2. **The tasksource container launders licences.**
   - `tasksource-instruct-v0` is tagged `apache-2.0` (and the GitHub repo CC BY 4.0). Both tags cover only the compilation.
   - Its per-task mirrors mislabel several sources:
     - dynahate is tagged `gpl`; the original is CC BY 4.0.
     - subjectivity is tagged `mit`; the original is CC BY 4.0.
     - SILICONE's own card says both `cc-by-sa-4.0` and BY-NC-SA.
     - Many are tagged `unknown`.
   - Provenance has to be taken from each original source, not from tasksource.
3. **No encoder has clean provenance.**
   - e5-large-v2 is MIT, but its training pairs are scraped (Reddit, Stack Exchange, Common Crawl), and it was fine-tuned on MS MARCO, whose terms say "non-commercial research purposes only".
   - jina-clip-v2 is **CC BY-NC 4.0**, and so is the code it pulls in with `trust_remote_code`. It cannot be in a commercial service without a licence from Jina.
   - Its image data traces to DataComp CommonPool, which contains passports, IDs and unblurred faces.
4. **Some sets already used fail too.**
   - The A5 "sweet" data (SST) has no licence and consists of copyrighted critic snippets.
   - The A5 "pictures" data (OASIS) is research-only. It has no per-image attribution, includes 346 photos of people, and paid raters about $1.80/h.
   - The A5 "junk" data (SMS Spam) includes real UK phone numbers and names in at least 33 messages.
   - Yelp's terms forbid commercial use and restrict publishing metrics. This matters for Jev-bench numbers and for DynaSent r1.
5. **The only clean sentiment and toxicity data is small or synthetic.**
   - Toxicity: DynaHate and HatemojiBuild are human-written adversarially, with pay of £16/h and welfare procedures documented. Civil Comments is CC0.
   - Sentiment: no openly licensed, consented set turned up. This is a real gap.
6. **Derived weights are committed.** `runs/a4-broad/real.pt`, `layered.pt` and the A4-pilot checkpoints are trained on kinds marked DROP here. If the v2 branch is public, it distributes models derived from that data.
7. **Not audited here:** the ~30 A4-pilot kinds outside the 44 (`runs/a4-pilot/report.log`, for example I2D2, SDOH-NLI, babi_nli, imppres, nli4ct), and the rest of the 429-task pool in `data/raw/a4/train_tasks.parquet`. They need the same pass before any reuse.

Wikipedia text paired with Wikidata classes or descriptions is the most promising clean source for the "label fits text" task:
- the text is volunteer-written and CC BY-SA;
- the labels are CC0;
- there is no crowd pay question;
- a pre-2022 dump keeps LLM text out.

## Corrections (2026-09-25, from fetching the originals; `docs/clean-data.md`)

- **FlyWire annotations:** Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.10877326 has no annotation table (only NBLAST
  scores and skeletons). The CC BY 4.0 copy is Nature's Supplementary Data 1, and it differs from the
  GitHub file the code uses: cell_type differs on 47,612 neurons. Used only to cross-check taste
  neurons, so it stays out of the product path until Nick decides.

  US only. The other four training genres (government, slate, telephone, travel) are fine.
- **DynaHate:** rounds 3 *and* 4 were adapted from real hateful posts. Use v0.2.3.
- **MASSIVE:** drop the `worker_id` column. **SNIPS:** use only the text and intent columns.
- **DBpedia-14 and BoolQ are share-alike:** redistributed derivatives carry the same licence.
- **Civil Comments and BoolQ** came from the Hugging Face copies under the Google org (the original
  links return 403). Civil Comments stays **REVIEW**: not trained on until commenter consent and rater
