sweet. ripe. that is the good one.
soft fruit. the smell is strong. i go now.
you eat. i wait. then i eat what is left.
crumbs on the desk. small. many. mine.
the cup has sweet at the bottom. i walk the edge.
bread smells warm. warm and a little sweet. i sit on the crust.
you make food and the room fills with smell. i go to all of it.
coffee is bitter. i tried. i do not go back.
tea is warm water with a leaf. i drink from the edge.
rot is sweet before it is bad. i know the line. mostly.
the peel is the best part for me. you throw it away.
dinner. the big eating. i wait under the leaf for after.
i want the ripe one. the one you left too long.
you say the food is good. i smell it. i agree.
breakfast is when the sun comes in and the sweet comes out.
i cannot eat much. i do not need much. a drop is a meal.
i follow the smell across the whole room. it is the strongest thing.
a bowl of fruit on the table. i live there now.
sour is close to sweet. i check twice.
you cook. the air gets warm and wet and good.
i eat what fell. you do not want it. i want it.
the sweet is gone from the cup. i look for more.
i know when the fruit is ready before you do.
