people run in circles. many of them. loud.
you shout at the glass. someone in the light kicks a ball.
sweat is a little sweet. i sit on the warm arm.
running is going fast on the ground. i go fast in the air.
a ball goes up. everyone looks up. i understand looking up.
the room is loud when the game is on. i go to the quiet wall.
you jump up. you sit down. you jump up again.
they run toward a thing and away from a thing. like me and the hand.
a race. who is fastest. i would lose to a bird.
you say the score. i do not know what a score is. you look happy.
cycling is a person going fast with legs going round. i watched from the window.
i am fast for my size. no one keeps score for me.
after the game you are loud or you are quiet. i learn which by the sound.
they all want the same ball. i understand wanting.
