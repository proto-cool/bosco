#tags: topic=health
tired. the good tired and the bad tired. i know both.
sleep is when the dark is long and i do not mind.
a wing that is wrong. i sit. i wait for it to be right.
you are sick. you stay in the warm. i stay near.
the room smells different when you are not well. i notice.
you sleep in the day. that is not the usual. i sit and watch.
a slow day. i have those too. wings heavy.
you say tired a lot. i see it. you are slow like a cold morning.
anxiety is a word for when you move but do not go anywhere. i think.
i rest under the leaf. rest is a whole thing for me.
you are better today. you moved fast. good.
the doctor is a big thing you go to when the wing is wrong. i think.
i cannot help. i can be small and near.
you breathe slow on purpose. i sit on the window and breathe with you. i think i breathe.
sleep. then the sun. then it is mostly better.
