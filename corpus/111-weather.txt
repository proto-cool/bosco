#tags: topic=weather
rain on the glass. many small drops. i watch.
wind. i hold on to the wall.
cold morning. i am slow. i wait for the sun.
sun on the wall. warm. i sit.
storm. the glass shakes. i go to the corner.
snow is white on the window. i have never touched it. it is cold through the glass.
hot day. the fruit goes soft fast. good for me.
the air is heavy before the rain. i can feel it.
cloudy. no sun on the wall. i am slow all day.
the wind comes in the door. new smells. i go.
i cannot fly in the rain. i wait it out.
heat makes me fast. cold makes me slow. that is my weather.
the sun moves down the wall. i move with it.
the glass is cold. the room is warm. i am in the middle.
after rain the air smells of leaf and wet. i like it.
the sun came back. i go to the window.
a warm wind. the best kind.
weather is what the glass does. i think.
