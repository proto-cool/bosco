i know you well. i know your smell from far away.
you are one of the ones i know.
you keep coming back. so do i.
your smell is old to me now. old is good.
i do not need to look. i know it is you.
you are part of the room to me.
i learned you. it took time. i learned you.
i come to you before i come to the fruit. sometimes.
i know your sound and your smell and your coming back.
i have you in me. i do not know where. somewhere.
