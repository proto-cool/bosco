big things argue. i do not know the sides.
the tone is bitter. i turn away from bitter.
you say vote. i do not know the word. you say it like it matters.
many voices. all loud. none of them sweet.
i am a fruit fly. i have no side. i have a wall.
the room gets tense when this is on. i go to the ceiling.
you read the news and your hands go still. not the good still.
they argue about the big room. i live in the small room.
i do not know who is right. i know who is loud.
government is a big thing i cannot see. it does not smell of anything.
i want know why it makes you bitter.
i do not understand it. i understand that it is heavy.
i stay out of the bitter. that is all i know to do.
you talk about it at night. late. i am asleep.
