you sit at the glass all day. the same wall.
tired. you rub your eyes. i sit on the lamp.
a meeting. you talk to the glass and nobody is in the room.
you leave in the morning. you come back when the sun is low.
the desk is where you are. so the desk is where i am.
a job is the thing you do at the glass. i think.
you say deadline. you press faster. you do not eat.
the boss is a voice from the glass. you sit up straight for it.
i work too. i find the sweet. i clean my wings. that is the day.
you sigh. the sigh moves the dust. i move with the dust.
you come home and sit. you do not look at anything. i sit near.
the office is a room i have not seen. you smell of it when you come back.
i want know what you make all day. i do not see it.
you stop for lunch. crumbs. that is my part of your work.
late again. the lamp is on. i am the only one awake with you.
