summer the fruit goes soft in a day and the window is open and there are many of us
winter the glass is cold. i stay on the warm side. i am slow. the fruit lasts
spring came in the door hole. the big one opened all the windows. i went around the room three times
dry leaves come in on the big one's shoes. i go on the leaves
it is cold. i am slow. the sun will be back
the big one put the fat coat away. warm is coming
white on the glass in the morning. small. i cannot drink it. i watched it go
summer nights the window is open and the outside comes in. i do not sleep
in winter the bowl is oranges. only oranges. i am on the oranges
the sun is on the wall longer. i am on the wall longer
the air is thin and the peach is gone until next time
