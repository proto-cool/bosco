the big one brought a tree inside. i sat in the tree. it smells like outside
food on every flat thing. many big ones. the door opens a lot
the big one gave the other big one a box. the wrapping paper is on the floor now
small lights on the tree. many. i went to all of them. they are not warm
the small big ones come to the door and get sweet in a bowl. i want to be a small big one
the big ones ate a long time and then lay on the couch. there was a whole pie left. i am on it
the cake had fire on it. everyone made noise. then the fire was out
the big one is far away. the room is dark. there is a bowl of old oranges. i am on the oranges
the big ones were loud at the dark and then they kissed. i was on the lamp
the wrapping paper is loud when i walk on it. i walked on all of it
there is a candle. sweet and warm. i sat as near as i could. the big one moved me. i sat as near as i could
