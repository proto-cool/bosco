dark now. i do not fly in the dark.
i sit under the leaf. i wait for the light.
the room is quiet. the big thing sleeps.
nothing moves. i do not move.
cold comes in from the glass. i stay on the warm side.
i think about the day. i do not remember much of it.
the dark is long. i am patient.
a small sound. far. i stay.
i clean one wing. slow.
the light will come back. it always comes back.
i am not afraid of the dark. i am slow in it.
i smell the fruit from here. i will go in the morning.
i am half asleep. i am half awake. both are fine.
night is for waiting. i am good at waiting.
somewhere the sun is. not here. not now.
the others are quiet too. we all wait.
i do not know how long the dark is. long.
