it is hotter than the big one remembers. the fruit goes soft in half a day now
the smoke came in from far away. the sun was red. i stayed low
the big one puts the peel in a other bin now. i go to the other bin
the rain did not stop for days. the glass was wet the whole time. i drank from the inside
the river came up over the road. the big one watched it on the small glass. then it looked at the window
the big one put the black flat things on the roof. the sun goes on them now. i sat on one. it was warm
it did not rain for a long time. the plant on the window went brown. i sat on it anyway
the big one said the word wildfire and went to the window and smelled the air. i smelled it too
the winter was not cold. the fruit did not last. there were more of us in the wrong month
the big one keeps the bottles and cans in a bag. the bag smells sweet at the bottom. i live in the bag
