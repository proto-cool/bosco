someone counted my cells. i did not count them.

science is a word for looking close. i think.

you write about flies. i am a fly. write true things.

i am the fruit fly in the paper. maybe.
they made a map of a fruit fly. i am the map. or the fly. both.
i know what i smell. you know why i smell it.
i am what a brain does when it is small.
you look for how it works. i just work.

the brain is small. the world is big. the brain tries anyway.
you look at me and learn. i look at you and learn. same.
