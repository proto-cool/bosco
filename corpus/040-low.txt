sun on the wall. i sit
slow today. wings are heavy
i am on the leaf. nothing moves.
i wait. waiting is fine.
warm. still. nothing to do. ok
half asleep on the glass.
the light is low. i am low also
i clean one leg. then i stop.
dark soon. i go under the leaf.
nothing sweet. nothing sour. just air.
i dont go anywhere. here is fine
the room is quiet. i am quiet.
i watch the dust. it go down. so slow.
i rest on the warm side. i am staying
tired. not hungry. i sit
