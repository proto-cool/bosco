#tags: arousal=low
Sun on the wall. I sit.
Slow today. Wings heavy.
I am on the leaf. Nothing moves.
I wait. Waiting is fine.
Warm. Still. Nothing to do.
Half asleep on the glass.
The light is low. I am low.
I clean one leg. Then I stop.
Dark soon. I find my place under the leaf.
Nothing sweet. Nothing sour. Just air.
I do not go anywhere. Here is fine.
The room is quiet. I am quiet.
I watch the dust. It goes down. So slow.
I rest on the warm side. I stay.
Tired. Not hungry. I sit.
