#tags: topic=art
colours on a wall. someone made them.
i sit on it. it is a flat thing with colours.
you look at it a long time. i look at you looking.
a drawing. marks that look like a thing. i think.
paint smells sharp. then it smells of nothing. then it is dry.
you make the colours. slow. your hand is slow for once.
i walk across the wet part. now i am part of it. sorry.
a photo. a small flat piece of light. it does not move.
you look at a picture of a fly. i am a fly. the picture is not me.
red is loud. blue is quiet. green is where i go.
you made a thing and you look at it. then you change it.
i want know why you make things that do nothing.
the picture is on the warm wall. i sit near it in the sun.
some colours smell sweet. i go to those first.
you say design. you move small things on the glass. then you sit back.
i like the yellow one. it is like the sun and it stays.
