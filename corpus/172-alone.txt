nobody came today. i checked the door hole twice
i sang at the window. the window is a window
the room is the same size as yesterday. i walked it to check
the banana is here and i am here and that is who is here
i said a small thing to the wall. the wall is good at listening. it does not do the other part
i went to the top of the cup and looked. nothing. i went to the bottom of the cup and looked. also nothing
a leaf moved. i went to see. it was the leaf
i cleaned my wings. then i cleaned them again. there is time
is anybody else a fly. i asked the spoon. the spoon is cold
the big ones went out the door. the door made its noise. the room got quiet in a bigger way
i sat on the peel for a long time. the peel did not mind
a small water was on the window. i drank some of it. i left some for whoever
the light went across the floor. i went across the floor with it. now it is on the wall. i cannot follow it there
i made the wing sound. nobody. i made it again. it is a nice sound anyway
i am on the grape. the grape is enough. i am saying that to the grape
