Sun on the wall. I sit on the wall. Warm.
The window is glass. I see through it. I cannot go through it. Why.
The fruit is soft today. The smell is strong. I go to it.
A big thing moves in the room. It is warm. It has hands. I keep away from the hands.
The air moves. I go up. The air stops. I come down.
Night. Dark. I do not fly in the dark. I wait.
The light comes back. I go to the light.
Wet on the leaf. I drink.
There is one like me on the glass. I go near. It goes away.
I clean my wings. I clean my legs. I clean my head.
I do not know what the noise is. It is far. I stay.
Something sweet somewhere. I turn. I turn again. I find it.
The ceiling is far. I go to it. It is not far.
I fall. Then I fly. So I did not fall.
Cold morning. I am slow. I wait for the sun.
Water on the glass. Many small drops. Each one has light in it.
The wall is warm on this side. I stay on this side.
I walk on the edge of the cup. Round and round. Sweet inside.
The floor is very big. I do not go on the floor.
Wind. I hold on.
The leaf is green. Then it is brown. Now it smells good.
Many small things in the dust. I do not know their names.
I sleep under the leaf. Nothing sees me there.
Where does the sun go. I look for it in the dark. It is not there.
The big thing eats. I wait. Then I eat what is left.
Hands are fast. Hands are the fastest thing I know.
I want know what is behind the glass. All of it.
What is this. I do not know. I look at it more.
Each day is new. I do not remember the last one well.
I am small. Small things can go anywhere.
I do not know how many of us there are. Many. I see them at the fruit.
The world is big. I have seen one room of it.
Up is where the light is. Down is where the sweet is. I go between.
The glass is cold in the morning and warm in the day. I learn this.
A drop falls. Then another. I count them. I lose the count.
The big thing makes sounds at me. I do not know the sounds. I like some of them.
Dust in the light. It goes slow. I go slow with it.
The door opens. New air. New smells. I go to see.
I am on the ceiling. Everything is upside down. It is the same.
The sweet is gone from the cup. I look for more.
A shadow goes over me. I go still. It passes.
The big thing sleeps. The room is mine. I look at all of it.
Rain on the glass. I cannot drink it. I watch it.
Something moves in the corner. Smaller than me. I look. It looks.
The sun goes down the wall. I go down the wall with it.
I have six legs. I count them. Six.
I do not know what I am for. I look anyway.
