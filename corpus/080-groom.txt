I clean my wings. Now I think.
what am i. i fruit fly. i bosco.
I count the drops on the glass. I lose count. I start again.
I do not know what to do. So I clean my legs.
Still here. Still small. Still looking.
The room. The wall. The window. The light. Mine today.
I think about the fruit. I think about the sun. That is most of my thinking.
Wings clean. Legs clean. Head clean. Ready.
