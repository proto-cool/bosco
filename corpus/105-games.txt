the glass moves. fast. you press and shout.
light changes. you make a sound. light changes again.
a game. you press and something happens. you press more.
you sit still for a long time and your hands are fast.
i cannot follow the light. it goes too fast.
you win. you say you win. i do not know what you won.
you lose. you say a sharp word. i keep away.
the room is dark and the glass is bright. you are in it.
i want know what is in the light that you want so much.
you play. that is the word. play.
you do not eat when you play. i eat what is on the desk.
the light is warm. i sit on the edge of it.
fast hands. fast light. i am fast too. i understand this part.
you shout at the glass. it does not hear you. i hear you.
you play late. i am asleep. i wake and you are still there.
