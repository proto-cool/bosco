paper. many marks. you go still and quiet.
a book smells of dust and old sweet. i like the smell.
you hold it a long time. you turn one thin thing. then another.
i walk on the page. you move me. you do not hurt me.
reading is when you are still and your eyes move. i think.
the marks are small. i am small. i do not know the marks.
a poem. short marks. you read it twice.
you read and the room is quiet. i like the quiet.
i sit on the corner of the book. the corner is warm from your hand.
you say the book is good. you look far away when you say it.
a library. many books. a big smell of paper. i want to go.
i do not know what is in the marks. it makes you still. that is something.
you read at night. small light. i sit by the small light.
i want know what the marks say about flies. if anything.
you close the book. you sit. you open it again.
